A clean verdict here is necessary, not sufficient. This page only sees what the browser is given, and CLX suppresses the keys it claims — press CLX+Z and the browser never sees a Z at all, only whatever CLX injected in its place. So a physical keypress can be invisible here while still reaching the engine and corrupting a measurement. The authoritative record is CLX's own hook log: every line with inj=false between startAt and endAt is a real keystroke that happened. Treat this page as the trigger and the first filter; treat the hook log as the proof.

Also keep it focused — a run that ends while the page is blurred is reported unverified rather than clean.
Timestamps are Date.now() epoch milliseconds — the same clock capslockx_hook.log and the SendInput harness stamp their lines with, so the window can be lined up against them directly.
